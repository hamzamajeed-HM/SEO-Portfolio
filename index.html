/about

A technical bias, applied to marketing.

I don't treat SEO and WordPress as separate disciplines. I use audits to find the problem, WordPress to ship the fix, and analytics to check my own work.

I'm an SEO Specialist and WordPress Webmaster with 4+ years sitting at the intersection of marketing and engineering, the person who can read a Screaming Frog crawl report in the morning and rebuild a WooCommerce checkout flow in the afternoon. Most of that time was spent as the sole SEO and web owner for Klimastal AS, a Norwegian industrial company, where I ran the full stack: technical audits, on-page and off-page strategy, content briefs, and the WordPress site itself.

Day to day, that means technical SEO audits, crawl errors, canonical conflicts, indexability issues, alongside Core Web Vitals work, schema implementation, and GA4 / Search Console reporting that stakeholders can actually act on. I've also built a 2,011-domain link-building prospect database from scratch, because off-page SEO for a B2B brand is a relevance problem, not a volume one. On the development side, I build and maintain WordPress and Elementor sites, from WooCommerce configuration to plain performance and responsiveness fixes.

In December 2025 I stepped away from that role to invest in a longer-term goal. Since January 2026 I've been focused full-time on Python, data analysis, and applied machine learning, completing an AI/ML and Deep Learning program at KIET (via NAVTTC) alongside Microsoft's Python and Data Visualization certificates, and building smaller projects along the way: a chatbot, a customer-churn prediction model, and a web-scraping automation pipeline. The plan is to bring that technical depth back into SEO and marketing work, and to take on Python or data-focused roles directly.

Klimastal AS is a Norwegian ventilation and HVAC equipment supplier. I owned SEO and the WordPress site for three years, treating technical audits, on-page copy and the WordPress build as one continuous job instead of three separate handoffs.

The problem

April 2022 baseline: mobile Performance score of 47/100 and a mobile LCP of 16.3s against Google's 2.5s threshold, on a site whose buyers search mostly on mobile. Desktop sat at 68/100. The live meta description ran 139 characters, under the 150-220 recommended range, and product pages read thin.

What I did

  • Scored every finding across four audit tools on impact versus effort before fixing anything
  • Led with image compression and format, the one issue every tool flagged independently
  • Rewrote and expanded homepage content in WordPress/Elementor across six product categories
  • Rewrote the title tag and meta description around commercial search intent
  • Re-tested performance at four checkpoints from April 2022 to October 2024
  • Built the 2,011-domain link-building database below from competitor and keyword research

Ziyana Yachts is a Dubai-based luxury yacht charter broker with a genuine worldwide charter network, a combination no local competitor or global directory can claim. I ran a full technical audit, competitive analysis and keyword-to-page roadmap. No CMS access here, this is analysis and a delivered recommendation set, a different skill from the Klimastal engagement above, and I've labeled it that way rather than blur the two together.

The problem

The site's highest-value page, the fleet listing, served Googlebot a "Loading yachts..." placeholder with zero crawlable content, on a page competing for "yacht charter Dubai" (6,000-9,000 monthly searches). No sitemap was discoverable, no structured data existed anywhere on the site, and mobile PageSpeed came back at 37/100.

What I did

  • Full crawl and indexation review: robots.txt, sitemap, canonical chains, meta robots
  • Root-caused the 20.6s mobile LCP to a booking widget's JS bundle plus 62+ synchronous requests
  • Competitive analysis against three Dubai operators and two global directories
  • Keyword-to-page map across five core pages with rewritten titles and meta descriptions
  • Delivered a prioritized 90-day roadmap and a before/target visibility scorecard

Off-page SEO for a Norwegian B2B ventilation supplier isn't a volume game, it's a relevance game. The goal wasn't to find 2,000 links, it was to find 2,000 plausible prospects across every credible outreach category so real outreach could be prioritized by authority and relevance instead of run blind.

What I did

  • Ran two parallel research methods, competitor-derived and keyword-derived
  • Merged and deduplicated results to 2,011 unique domains
  • Categorized every domain by source type: general, blog, wiki, forum, news, review
  • Flagged the 86-domain wiki/forum/news/review tier as the best authority-to-effort starting point
